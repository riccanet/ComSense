What and when

Upgrades are queued only on firewalls whose last update check found one, and each is preceded by a configuration backup. A firewall that is offline at the start gets its task if it comes back within the window; after that the task expires, so nothing upgrades in the middle of the working day. Upgrading pfSense reboots the firewall.
